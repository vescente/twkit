## Gaps: Tableau writes the attribute in our context and we do not

Required by the XSD, present in real workbooks in the SAME context, missing in ours: check these first.

- `<filter kind>` — in `<view>` 17 of 574
  · **resolved:** only on hide filters (`kind='hide'`, 17 of 574): appears when the user hides individual values by hand. Not needed by the generator.

## XSD false alarms

Marked required, but Tableau itself does not write it in OUR context: either the schema is wider than reality or the attribute belongs to a same-named element under another parent.

- `<relation expression>`
- `<column delta-type>`
- `<column field>`
- `<column ordinal>` (only inside `columns`)
- `<members value>`
- `<worksheet version>`
- `<view value>`
- `<rows delta-type>`
- `<rows field>`
- `<filter alt-text>`
- `<filter field>`
